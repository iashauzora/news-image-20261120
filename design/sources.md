## 2. Cyclone Akvan makes landfall on India's east coast

Cyclone Akvan crossed the north Andhra Pradesh–south Odisha coast close to
Gopalpur on Friday morning, with winds of 70–80 km/h gusting to 90 km/h —
India's third cyclone landfall in less than a month. Odisha evacuated over
300,000 people; no major damage was reported.

- Reuters, 20 November 2026 — "India's eastern coast hit by third cyclone
  within a month": heavy rain battered the coast as Akvan made landfall, IMD
  statement, no damage reported so far.
- The Hindu BusinessLine, live updates, 20 November 2026 — landfall process
  commenced near Gopalpur around 8 am; crossed by 8:30 am at 70–80 km/h
  gusting to 90 km/h; over 3 lakh evacuated, 9,341 shelters.
- Jagran Josh, IMD Weather Today, November 20, 2026 — IMD's 20 Nov bulletin:
  landfall along the north AP–south Odisha coasts near Gopalpur.
